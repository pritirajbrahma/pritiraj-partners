One discipline, two ventures

A promise only counts when it arrives.

For seven years I have worked on one question: why do promises fail between the announcement and the last mile? It ran through campaigns in six states, two years inside the Government of India, and every essay I publish. East & Ember asks the same question of food, where the promise is made on an app and kept, or broken, at the door.

Politics is the promise. Governance is the delivery. So is a kitchen.The idea behind both ventures
