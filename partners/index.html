A nation is judged by what reaches the last home.

Politics is the promise. Governance is the delivery. Pritiraj & Partners advises those who govern, and those who seek to, on closing the distance between the two.

The firm

Partner-led, or not at all. The partner who wins your confidence in the first meeting leads the fieldwork, writes the memorandum, and sits across the table when the decision is taken. The firm takes selected mandates on a project basis, and the names attached to past mandates are never disclosed.

Mandates won in Rajasthan, West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Karnataka. Standing electoral intelligence for the North East, Rajasthan and West Bengal.

The Practice in full

Instruments

Delivery DiagnosticFor governments. A ground-truthed assessment of one flagship scheme: where it leaks between sanction and beneficiary, why, and a costed correction plan. One scheme, 6–8 weeks, fixed fee stated upfront.
Constituency IntelligenceFor leaders and candidates. What voters are saying, where the organisation is thin, and the ninety days of work that would change both. One constituency, six months minimum, fixed fee.
Last Mile IndexA stage-by-stage, field-verified measure of scheme delivery across Indian states, published quarterly. Sample edition.

Method

  1. Ground truth. Weeks 1–4. Field study, opposition and incumbency analysis, and document review against the ground.
  2. Strategy. Weeks 5–8. Options priced and sequenced, ending in a written recommendation.
  3. Architecture. Weeks 9–12. A 30–60–90 day plan, monitoring design and review cadence.
  4. Review. Onward. Reviews against the plan’s own exit criteria, documented in writing.

Ethics charter

No deceptionNo voter deception, no coordinated inauthentic behaviour, no communal or divisive messaging, at any fee.
One side onlyNo simultaneous mandates for direct electoral competitors. A conflicts register is checked before every proposal.
Lawful, in writingFull compliance with the Model Code of Conduct and election law. Counsel is documented in writing.
No outcome feesFees are never contingent on electoral outcomes.
