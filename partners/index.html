The Practice in full

Instruments

Delivery Diagnostic
For governments. A ground-truthed assessment of one flagship scheme: where it leaks between sanction and beneficiary, why, and a costed correction plan. One scheme, 6–8 weeks, fixed fee stated upfront.
Constituency Intelligence
For leaders and candidates. What voters are saying, where the organisation is thin, and the ninety days of work that would change both. One constituency, six months minimum, fixed fee.
Last Mile Index
A stage-by-stage, field-verified measure of scheme delivery across Indian states, published quarterly. Sample edition.

Method · Twelve weeks from ground truth to a plan

  1. Weeks 1–4Ground truth

    Field study, opposition and incumbency analysis, and document review against the ground.

  2. Weeks 5–8Strategy

    Options priced and sequenced, ending in a written recommendation.

  3. Weeks 9–12Architecture

    A 30–60–90 day plan, monitoring design and review cadence.

  4. OnwardReview

    Reviews against the plan’s own exit criteria, documented in writing.

Ethics charter

No deception
No voter deception, no coordinated inauthentic behaviour, no communal or divisive messaging, at any fee.
One side only
No simultaneous mandates for direct electoral competitors. A conflicts register is checked before every proposal.
Lawful, in writing
Full compliance with the Model Code of Conduct and election law. Counsel is documented in writing.
No outcome fees
Fees are never contingent on electoral outcomes.
