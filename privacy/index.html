Privacy Policy

How Pritiraj & Partners handles the information you share with us, and the obligations we hold ourselves to as a firm that works on matters of public consequence.

Pritiraj & Partners ("the firm", "we") is a governance and political strategy advisory practice based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you may ask us to do with it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

Information you give us. If you write to us about a mandate, a speaking request, or The Pritiraj Review, we receive whatever you choose to send — typically your name, your email address, your organisation, and the substance of your enquiry. We do not operate a subscription form, a login, or a payment facility on this website.

Information collected automatically. Like any hosted website, this site records standard technical data: IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and pages visited. This is used in aggregate to understand which essays are read and whether the site works properly. We do not build advertising profiles and we run no advertising trackers.

What we do not collect. We do not collect sensitive personal data through this website — no financial information, no government identifiers, no health or biometric data. Please do not send such material by email either; if a mandate requires it, we will agree a secure channel with you first.

How we use it

We use what you send us to answer you, to assess whether a mandate is one we can properly take, and to maintain our own records of professional correspondence. We use aggregate site data to improve the site and to understand which questions our readers care about. We do not use your information to train models, and we do not use it for any purpose you would not reasonably expect from the enquiry you sent.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data. We do not rent, trade or share mailing lists. We do not disclose the identity of a prospective client, or the fact that an approach was made, to anyone outside the firm.

That last point deserves emphasis, because of the work we do. An approach to this firm is confidential from the moment it is made, whether or not it becomes an engagement. The firm's Ethics Charter governs how we handle conflicts, and it applies to enquiries as much as to signed mandates.

Who else sees it

Your data may be processed by the service providers that make this site and our correspondence work — our website host and our email provider. They act on our instructions and are permitted to use the data only to provide those services. We may disclose information where we are required to by Indian law, by a court, or by a regulator with jurisdiction over us; we would seek to notify you first unless prohibited from doing so.

How long we keep it

Correspondence relating to an enquiry that does not become a mandate is retained for up to two years, so that we can identify conflicts and recognise repeat approaches. Records relating to an engagement are retained for the period required by our professional and statutory obligations. Aggregate site analytics are retained in non-identifying form.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to confirm what personal data of yours we hold, to correct it if it is wrong or incomplete, to erase it where we have no continuing obligation to retain it, and to withdraw a consent you have given. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity. We will respond to any such request within a reasonable period, and we will not charge you for making one.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies necessary for it to function and for basic, aggregate usage measurement. It carries no advertising cookies and no third-party marketing pixels. Your browser can refuse cookies; the site will continue to work if you do.

Children

This site is intended for a professional readership and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under eighteen.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the revised version will be published here with a new effective date. We will not apply a materially different use to information already collected without asking you first.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests concerning your data, should go to Pritiraj Brahma, Founding Partner. The firm is based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
