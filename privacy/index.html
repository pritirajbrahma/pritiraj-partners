Legal

Privacy, plainly.

What this site handles about you, and what you may ask of us.

  1. I

    Whose site this is

    This website belongs to Pritiraj Brahma. It presents two organisations Pritiraj built: Pritiraj & Partners, a governance and political strategy advisory practice, and East & Ember Hospitality Group. The two are separate entities. This policy covers this website only, and explains what personal data it handles, why, and what you may ask of us. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

  2. II

    What we collect

    What you send us. The enquiry forms on this site do not submit or store anything. When you complete one, your own email app opens with your answers set out, and nothing reaches us until you press send. We then receive what you chose to include: typically your name, email address, organisation and the substance of your enquiry. There is no login, subscription or payment facility on this site.

    Technical data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server data such as IP address, browser and pages requested, under its own policy. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads. We run no analytics, no advertising trackers and no marketing pixels.

    What we do not collect. No financial information, government identifiers, health or biometric data. Please do not send such material by email; if a matter requires it, we will agree a secure channel with you first.

  3. III

    How we use it

    We use what you send to answer you. An investor enquiry is handled for the East & Ember Hospitality Group raise; a mandate enquiry is handled by Pritiraj & Partners to assess whether it is a mandate we can properly take; anything else is answered directly. We keep records of professional correspondence. We do not use your information to train models, and we do not use it for any purpose you would not reasonably expect from the enquiry you sent.

  4. IV

    What we never do

    We do not sell personal data, and we do not rent, trade or share mailing lists. We do not disclose the identity of a prospective client or investor, or the fact that an approach was made, to anyone outside the entity it was addressed to. An approach is confidential from the moment it is made, whether or not it leads anywhere. The Pritiraj & Partners Ethics Charter governs how conflicts are handled, and applies to enquiries as much as to signed mandates.

  5. V

    Who else sees it

    Your data may be processed by the providers that make this site and our correspondence work: the website host and our email provider. They act on our instructions and may use the data only to provide those services. We may disclose information where required by Indian law, by a court, or by a regulator with jurisdiction; we would seek to notify you first unless prohibited from doing so.

  6. VI

    How long we keep it

    Correspondence about an enquiry that does not proceed is kept for up to two years, so that conflicts and repeat approaches can be recognised. Records relating to an engagement or an investment are kept for the period our professional and statutory obligations require.

  7. VII

    Your rights

    Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to confirm what personal data of yours we hold, to correct it, to erase it where we have no continuing obligation to keep it, and to withdraw a consent you have given. You may nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity. We will respond within a reasonable period and will not charge you for a request.

  8. VIII

    Cookies and children

    This site sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser. It is intended for a professional readership and is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under eighteen.

  9. IX

    Changes and contact

    If this policy changes materially, the revised version will be published here with a new effective date, and we will not apply a materially different use to information already collected without asking you first. Questions about this policy, or requests about your data, can be sent through the contact page, addressed to Pritiraj Brahma.
