Business

East & Ember operates its own kitchens rather than licensing a menu, so every order is cooked to one standard by a team it trains.

DeliveryNortheast Indian food from company-owned cloud kitchens, sold through Swiggy and Zomato.
TiffinA weekday lunch tiffin for Bengaluru’s technology parks, built for repeat orders.
PicklesA packaged pickles line that extends the kitchen beyond its delivery radius.

Plan

  1. Seed round. Two Bengaluru kitchens, a dedicated tiffin kitchen and a pickle facility.
  2. Delhi NCR. Expansion from FY2029.
  3. Dining. Restaurant formats later, in Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Guwahati.

Questions

What is East & Ember Hospitality Group?

East & Ember Hospitality Group is building India’s Northeast Indian food platform. It serves delivery-first Northeast Indian food from company-owned kitchens, with a weekday lunch tiffin for tech parks and a pickles line.

Who founded East & Ember?

East & Ember was founded by Pritiraj Brahma, who is from Assam. Pritiraj is also Founding Partner of Pritiraj & Partners and the author of The Pritiraj Review.

Where is East & Ember based?

East & Ember is based in Bengaluru. Its pilot kitchen is in Electronic City, and it plans to expand to Delhi NCR from FY2029.

How has the pilot performed?

Between May and October 2026 the pilot kitchen took 3,603 orders on Swiggy and Zomato with no paid advertising. Between 49% and 60% of orders came from repeat customers, and the kitchen held a 4.24 rating on Zomato in October 2026.
