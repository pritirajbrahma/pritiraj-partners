About

Pritiraj Brahma

Founder, East & Ember Hospitality Group
Founding Partner, Pritiraj & Partners

Pritiraj Brahma builds organisations that deliver on what they promise. Over seven years he has worked on both sides of public life: five years in national-party campaigns, with electoral mandates designed and won booth by booth in six states, and two years inside the Government of India. He studied Public Policy and Management at Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB).

He founded Pritiraj & Partners to advise those who govern on closing the distance between promise and delivery, and East & Ember Hospitality Group to bring the same operating discipline to food, beginning with Northeast Indian cuisine from company-owned kitchens in Bengaluru.

Questions

Who is Pritiraj Brahma?

Pritiraj Brahma is an Indian political strategist and entrepreneur from Assam, based in Bengaluru. Pritiraj is Founding Partner of Pritiraj & Partners, a political consulting and government delivery advisory, and founder of East & Ember Hospitality Group, which is building India’s Northeast Indian food platform.

What does Pritiraj Brahma do as a political consultant?

Pritiraj designs election campaigns and the delivery instruments that follow them: seat-level arithmetic, war-room design, field research and sentiment tracking, manifesto and narrative architecture, and delivery advisory for governments. The work starts from large volumes of electoral and field data and turns them into a plan a campaign or a government can act on.

Where has Pritiraj Brahma won mandates?

Over five years of national-party campaigns, Pritiraj designed campaigns that won electoral mandates booth by booth in six states: Rajasthan, West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Karnataka.

Which regions does Pritiraj Brahma work in?

Pritiraj & Partners works from Bengaluru across South India, in Delhi and the National Capital Region, and in Guwahati and the eight states of the North East, where it keeps a standing repository of electoral intelligence.

Where did Pritiraj Brahma study?

Pritiraj studied Public Policy and Management at Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB), and spent two years delivering policy inside the Government of India, including the World Bank-funded SANKALP programme with the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.

What is East & Ember Hospitality Group?

East & Ember Hospitality Group, founded by Pritiraj Brahma, is building India’s Northeast Indian food platform from company-owned kitchens in Bengaluru. It is raising its Seed round.
