Pritiraj Brahma

Founder, East & Ember Hospitality Group. Founding Partner, Pritiraj & Partners. Author, The Pritiraj Review. Bengaluru, from Assam.

Summary

Pritiraj Brahma works on one problem: why commitments fail between announcement and delivery, and how organisations are built so that they do not. That question has run through five years of national-party campaigns, two years inside the Government of India, and the essays of The Pritiraj Review.

East & Ember Hospitality Group applies it to food. Food businesses in India fail on execution more than demand: consistency as kitchens multiply, licensing and compliance across states, and customers who return without being paid to. These are delivery problems.
