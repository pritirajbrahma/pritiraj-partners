Profile

Pritiraj Brahma

Founder, East & Ember Hospitality Group
Founding Partner, Pritiraj & Partners

Pritiraj Brahma builds organisations that deliver on what they promise. Over seven years he has worked on both sides of public life: five years in national-party campaigns, with electoral mandates designed and won in six states, and two years inside the Government of India as a Mahatma Gandhi National Fellow from IIM Bangalore.

He founded Pritiraj & Partners to advise those who govern on closing the distance between promise and delivery, and East & Ember Hospitality Group to bring the same operating discipline to food, beginning with Northeast Indian cuisine from company-owned kitchens in Bengaluru.
