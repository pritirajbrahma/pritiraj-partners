The story

Seven years on one question.

Why do promises fail between the announcement and the last mile, and what does it take to make them arrive? That question ran through five years of national-party campaigns and winning mandates in six states, and through two years inside the Government of India’s Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship as a Mahatma Gandhi National Fellow at IIM Bangalore.

East & Ember asks it of food. The Northeast’s cuisine is loved by those who know it and almost absent from India’s delivery apps. Food businesses here fail on execution more than appetite: consistency as kitchens multiply, licences across states, and customers who come back without being paid to. Those are delivery problems.
