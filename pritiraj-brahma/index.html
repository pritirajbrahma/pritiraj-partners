01 The argument

Politics is the promise.
Governance is the delivery.

A mandate is won on what a leader undertakes and judged on what a citizen receives. Almost all of the machinery between those two facts is invisible to the voter and unexamined by the commentary — which is precisely why it fails without consequence.

02 The argument

Failure is architectural,
not motivational.

The same defects recur across sectors and across states: a task with no named owner, a target with no deadline, a result nobody is obliged to publish. Ambition is rarely the missing input. An owner, a date and a published number usually are.

03 The argument

What is not measured
is not delivered.

Where a programme publishes only its disbursement and never its outcome, the outcome is unmanaged by construction. The absence of a number is not an administrative gap — it is a decision, and decisions can be reversed.

04 The argument

A nation is judged by
what reaches its last home.

Not by what is sanctioned, not by what is inaugurated, and not by what a dashboard reports. The test of a state is the household at the end of the line, and that household is where the evidence has to be gathered.

The argument

Indian public life is good at announcement and weak at arrival. The distance between them is a design problem, and design problems are fixable by people willing to name them. Four propositions organise everything the firm publishes — open any one.

How the Review is made

The Review is a by-product of the firm’s practice, not a separate research operation. That is the whole explanation for its cadence — open any one.

The standing rule

Every figure is attributed to a named source. Where no primary document exists, the essay says so. Nothing is asserted that the author has not checked against the document it came from.

Source How the Review is made

Where essays come from

Reading the ground picture on government initiatives is daily work, and produces a daily note for the leaders the firm advises. An essay begins only where a pattern has held across states and years — the research precedes the essay, and never the other way round.

Standard How the Review is made

How figures are verified

Primary document first; otherwise an official release from MoSPI, the RBI or the NSS, a PIB or ministry note, or a parliamentary reply. Two independent sources wherever practicable. Reporting is labelled as reporting, never dressed as a statistic.

Conflict How the Review is made

Disagreement is not resolved silently

Where credible sources conflict, the essay carries both numbers and says which it relies on and why. Choosing one quietly is the more common practice and the less honest one.

Tools How the Review is made

What AI does and does not do

The arguments and the words are the author’s. AI assists in structuring an argument once its evidence is assembled, and built this site. It is never used to source facts: every figure is verified by the author against the document it came from.

Corrections. A publication that demands accountability of the state owes the same standard. An error here will be corrected, the essay marked as revised with the date, and the correction will say what changed rather than quietly replacing it. Every essay carries its publication and last-revision date, and a data-vintage note where the argument rests on a dated series.
