About

Pritiraj Brahma

Founder, East & Ember Hospitality Group
Founding Partner, Pritiraj & Partners

Pritiraj Brahma builds organisations that deliver on what they promise. Over seven years he has worked on both sides of public life: five years in national-party campaigns, with electoral mandates designed and won booth by booth in six states, and two years inside the Government of India. He studied Public Policy and Management at IIMB (Indian Institute of Management Bangalore).

He founded Pritiraj & Partners to advise those who govern on closing the distance between promise and delivery, and East & Ember Hospitality Group to bring the same operating discipline to food, beginning with Northeast Indian cuisine from company-owned kitchens in Bengaluru.

The record

East & Ember Hospitality Group, Bengaluru. Pilot kitchen in Electronic City since May 2026: 3,603 Swiggy and Zomato orders to October 2026 with no paid advertising, 49–60% of orders from repeat customers, and a 4.24 rating on Zomato in October.

Founding Partner, Pritiraj & Partners. Five years in national-party campaigns, with electoral mandates designed and won in Rajasthan, West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Karnataka.

Writing
