The Practice

Political strategy for those who seek a mandate. Government delivery advisory for those who hold one. One method underneath both, because the two are the same question read at different hours.

Politics is the promise. Governance is the delivery. India's failures are failures of institutional design — tasks without owners, targets without deadlines, results nobody is required to publish — not failures of ambition or resources. A nation is judged not by what it announces, but by what reaches its last home.

Pritiraj & Partners is a political consulting and governance advisory firm working with India's political and governing leadership. It was founded on a narrow observation: the distance between a manifesto sentence and a delivered outcome is rarely closed by more money or more will. It is closed by an instrument — a named owner, a dated target, and a number somebody is obliged to publish. Most of what the firm does is build those instruments, or build the campaign that earns the right to.

Four kinds of mandate

Governance and delivery

Delivery diagnostics on flagship schemes; programme-management-unit design inside a chief minister's or a minister's office; last-mile monitoring built from field evidence rather than from a dashboard's self-report. The test of this work is not a report. It is whether the government can publish its own delivery numbers, accurately, after the firm has left the room.

Political strategy

Campaign strategy, war-room design, candidate selection, seat-level arithmetic and alliance analysis. The firm's founding partner has designed winning campaigns in six states. What travels between them is the method — read the terrain, find the argument that terrain will actually accept, then build the machinery to carry it. What does not travel is the argument itself.

Field research and public listening

Constituency surveys, sentiment tracking and structured listening. This exists because the alternative — governing from the aggregate — is how a state discovers a failure two years after the household did.

Leadership and narrative

Positioning, speech and manifesto architecture, and counsel in a crisis. A manifesto written as a delivery document, with owners and dates against each commitment, is a harder document to write and a much harder one to break.

Where we work

The firm reads four terrains in institutional detail rather than claiming national coverage it could not honour. Each has a note of its own: Bengaluru, the National Capital Territory of Delhi, Guwahati, and the eight states of the North East.

Questions we are asked

What does a political consulting firm actually do?

Two different things, usually conflated. Before a mandate, it builds the argument, the coalition arithmetic and the ground machinery that win it. After a mandate, it builds the instruments that deliver on it — owners for tasks, deadlines for targets, and a published record of results. Pritiraj & Partners takes both kinds of work, and treats them as one method: politics is the promise, governance is the delivery.

Do you advise parties, governments, or individual candidates?

All three, and never more than one interest in the same contest. Governments and their departments take delivery mandates. Parties and candidates take strategy mandates. The firm declines a brief where advising it would compromise a mandate already held.

Which states does the firm work in?

The founding partner has designed winning campaigns in six states. In North East India the firm has won mandates in Assam, Meghalaya and Arunachal Pradesh and is engaged in Nagaland, and it maintains a standing repository of electoral intelligence across all eight states. The four terrains it reads in institutional detail are Bengaluru, the National Capital Territory of Delhi, Guwahati, and the eight states of the North East. Work elsewhere in India begins with reading the terrain, not with reusing a playbook from another one.

Is this campaign management or governance advisory?

Both, kept separate. A campaign mandate is time-boxed to a cycle and judged on a result. A delivery mandate is judged on whether a scheme reaches its last intended household, and on whether the government can publish that fact without the firm in the room.

Do you take mandates outside election season?

Most of the firm's work sits outside it. The distance between an announcement and a delivered outcome is a year-round problem, and it is the harder one.

What does the firm publish?

The Pritiraj Review — long-form, evidence-led essays on how India is governed and how it delivers, published most weeks since 1 February 2026. Every figure is attributed to the instrument that produced it. The Civics Series, a free five-chart reference on how the Union and a state are actually structured, sits alongside it.

How does a mandate begin?

With a written note from you and a conversation with the founding partner. The firm scopes the question before it quotes the work, and says so when the question does not need a consultant.

What we will not do

The firm does not take two interests in one contest. It does not manufacture a ground report to fit a leader's expectation, which is the most commercially tempting thing in this trade and the fastest way to lose a state. It does not run communications it would be unwilling to see quoted. And it says when a question does not need a consultant, because the firm's argument is that most delivery failures are design failures a government can fix itself once someone names them.
